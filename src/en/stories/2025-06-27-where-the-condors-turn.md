## I. The thermal

Then the wind turns. You feel it first on your face, a warm breath rising out of the canyon, and a moment later the first bird lets go of the rock. It does not flap. It simply opens, three metres of black and white, and the air carries it up past the railing so close that you can hear the feathers.

[photo 2 3]

An old woman from Cabanaconde, selling knitted hats, told me that the condor carries the souls of the dead up to the sun. I did not believe her, and then, watching the fourth bird climb into the light without a single beat of its wings, I was no longer sure.
