You lie behind the non-existent line that separates a fierce storm from tranquility. The colossals tickle the board beneath you as they expand and explode in front of you. The sea shakes and howls, yet in the midst of chaos, you lie at peace.

...

The unsuspecting spectator gazes at the distant dots in the sea. Far away from the shore, the sea warriors are preparing to challenge the unforgiving giants. *Sink or swim. Live or die.* The ferocious battle tends to discourage the average watcher; however, the persistent sea soldiers keep at it. Win or lose, they bravely paddle back into the eye of the storm, ready to once again face the ruthless beasts.

“I wish that was me,” you think to yourself.

You stand in awe as this theatre of life shows off its finest actors on the most esteemed of stages. Your soul screams, your body itching for movement. You are tired of only spectating. For far too long have your feet been sand-bound, frozen and stuck at the thought of being left at the mercy of fate. *Sink or swim. Live or die.* Your feet move slowly towards the sea, carrying the weight of doubt—yet still, they move.

A brave, distant figure challenges the wave. He gracefully moves alongside the all-swallowing force on a colorful board, dancing to the rhythm of the tide. How does one domesticate these savages? You imagine brushing your fingers through the raging waves, stuck in the moment—becoming one with nature.

Unexpectedly, the persistent giant gains size and begins collapsing over the skillful sea dancer, shattering your daydream to pieces. A thunderous blast strikes the sea, leaving only vast white foam to tell the story of what once was. Yet again, fear and anxiety gain control of you as your feet dig deeper into the sand. The fierce giant approaches you, transforming smoothly into a gentle ripple; but make no mistake, it is the same beast who had devoured that graceful dancer just a moment ago. With a tender push, the soft ripple momentarily releases your feet from the muddy sand, and with it, brings a present—the colorful board, which now rests against your shins.

The sudden surprise stuns you. You have been presented with an opportunity. What will you do?

*When God wanted to make a king out of David, He didn’t bring him a crown. He brought him Goliath.*

The sea soldier, visible to you only as a distant dot, reappears from the rowdy sea, accompanied by another vague dot in the endless ocean. They gesture vaguely, calling upon you to join them. Now is the time to be defiant in the face of your foul cowardice. *Sink or swim. Live or die.*

All eyes are on you, you feel. Their piercing gazes smear your lungs with pressure. *Breathe*. Your legs begin to shiver as the air around you thickens. *Close your eyes.*

“The water is awfully cold today,” you think to yourself.

The sun is burning your guts as the board continues banging on your shins. *Breathe*. The distant screams from the beach rape your eardrums. All the noise, all the stares, all the pressure seem to be directed at you, and you alone. *Breathe. Relax. Breathe. Remember. Breathe. Breathe. Breathe...*

“I’ve been here before,” you affirm as your eyes open.

While the wind whispers secrets to the worthy, you grab hold of the board and place your scorched belly on it. A stream of energy is bursting through your body; the mind as clear as ever. The water, however, is still cold. You propel yourself towards the expanding dots, noticing the vague appearance of muscles in your back that had never been used before.

The dots cease to be, and instead, two long-haired sea soldiers appear in front of you, wearing foreign smiles on their faces. They have seen you before. They notice the resolute gleam in your eyes as you begin to float adrift. A hand calms your movement, the whitewash blessing you with a temporary stillness of the sea.

“You cannot control how the sea acts,” explains one of the sea soldiers, still carrying the foreign grin.

But how does one tame a beast?

Before you manage to translate your thoughts into words, the sea dancer previously devoured by the savage wave answers your question, as if he had read your mind. Remember, he’s seen you before.

“You can only react to it, brother. Trust your heart,” he adds, with a piercingly compassionate gaze.

While you float, digesting their strange wisdom, the sea begins trembling as the giants make their way towards you, closing the distance at a raging pace.

Have I been here before? Have they seen me before? Whatever the case may be, you decide to trust your intuition. Or the heart*? Relax. Breathe.* Or whatever that echoing voice is.

A firm push from behind unexpectedly sends you towards the giants. The sea dancer paddles by your side. He leads the way confidently, cutting the distance, and as he approaches the beast, he turns his head towards you and shouts:

“Time your dive!”

The graceful dancer sends a wide smile your way before he dives with his board under the raging wave. The torrent stomps over him as you make eye contact with the beast.

“You’re next,” says the beast.

In this eternal second, you float in disbelief as the Goliath saturates your view, devouring the bright blue sky completely, unapologetically. The crushing surge heaves all its weight to the top, ready to collapse over you with all its might.

Heartbeats pounding through your chest, every muscle in your body coiled like a spring. You are swallowed by the shadow of what’s to come*. Sink or swim. Live or die.* An indistinct reflex takes hold of your body as the signal goes off in your head.

Now!

Mimicking the motion you have observed many times before, you dive beneath the sea, tightly squeezing the board. Eyes closed, a deep breath rests in your lungs as all above-surface sound is deafened. The pressure of the mighty surge sharply passes through you, stretching your skin like a rapid rollercoaster ride. Your body clenches the board, veins popping from all sides. Now, you are at the mercy of fate.

Surprisingly, for you, it’s not fear or anxiety that appear; instead, you are overcome by exhilaration. Sink or swim, you have bravely challenged your Goliath, and for such an endeavor, the sea gods have blessed you. The wave pressingly rolls over your feet, sending the upper part of your body back to the surface. As a wild smile usurps your salty face, you shift your gaze back towards the Goliath, shrinking in size as it heads for shore, towards the distant dots at the beach.

A moment of inattentiveness can be expensive, you realize, as the board trembles, reminding you that the beast never comes alone. Light on the board, you catch the last available moment to roll under once again, managing to dodge what would have been a critical blow. The beast slips behind you as you face the barrage of incoming waves, hungry for your indecisiveness.

In the corner of your salted eyes, you spot the sea dancer still gracefully moving slightly ahead of you, seemingly unaware of your presence. He is a blueprint for your success; a ray of light ready to guide you to the lineup. The sea continues sending powerful walls of water your way, each one tougher on your burnt-up body than the last. However, the water doesn’t feel so cold anymore. The muscles in your back burn and ache as they exceed their limits with each determined paddle of your arms through heavy water. There is no going back. Digging deep inside yourself to find the power to cut through the last bit of obstacle standing in your way, you come to a strange understanding. The beasts are nowhere around, nor have they ever been. In front of you, there are only waves. Powerful, wild; yet still, just waves. You smoothly glide over the last row of them, managing to glide over their shoulders even before they break.

Finally, you have reached the small group of restful surfers, peacefully lying on their boards, the waves unable to do anything but shake them. You have reached the lineup.

...

In the midst of chaos, you found peace. The line separating a fierce storm from the tranquil hideaway where you lie unbothered. Is that line within or without? The grins of the sea soldiers are not so foreign; the wind whispers that you now wear a similar one as well. A mellow zephyr caresses your wet face as your eyes lock in on the distant horizon, where the golden sun is slowly setting. The mind has disconnected, leaving your heart in charge. The current softly pushes your board and turns your gaze towards the shore. Far away from the lineup, distant spectators gaze unsuspectingly. The surface shakes slightly as you hear the sea spirits calling your name once again. Are you ready to dance?
