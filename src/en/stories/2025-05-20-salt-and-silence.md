## I. The mirror

It had rained the night before. When the jeep stopped and the engine went quiet, the salt was covered in a thin skin of water, and the sky had come down to lie on it. The horizon disappeared. Clouds floated under our feet. The driver, who had seen this a thousand times, lit a cigarette and looked at it anyway.

[photo 1]

Nobody spoke. Silence on the altiplano is not an absence; it has weight and temperature, it presses on the ears. I stood in the middle of it and understood, for the first time on the whole journey, how small a person is, and how strangely comforting that can be.
